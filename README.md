# 🛒 Cart2Insights: Decoding E-Commerce Performance

## Step 1: Understand the Business Problem

### Business Context

The e-commerce platform generates data related to customers, orders, products, sellers, payments, deliveries, and customer reviews.

The purpose of this project is to analyze this data and understand overall business performance.

The complete workflow includes:

- Data Understanding
- Data Quality Analysis
- Data Cleaning
- SQL Analysis
- Feature Engineering
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Interactive Streamlit Dashboard

### Key Business Objectives

- Understand sales and revenue performance
- Analyze customer behavior
- Identify top products and sellers
- Evaluate delivery performance
- Understand customer satisfaction

### Questions the Analysis Should Answer

1. What is the total revenue generated by the business?
2. How many orders, customers, and sellers are there?
3. How does revenue change month by month?
4. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
5. Which sellers generate the highest revenue?
6. Which states have the highest customer spending?
7. How many customers are new and repeat customers?
8. Who are the top customers by spending?
9. What is the average delivery time?
10. How many orders are delivered on time or delayed?
11. Which locations have slower delivery performance?
12. Does delivery delay affect customer review scores?
13. What is the distribution of customer review scores?
14. Which categories receive the most customer reviews?
15. Do delayed orders receive lower ratings than on-time orders?

## Step 2: Understand the Dataset & ER Diagram

The project contains 9 related e-commerce datasets. Each table stores a different type of business information.

### Understand the 9 Tables

This project contains 9 related e-commerce datasets.

1. Customers :- Stores customer information such as customer ID, unique customer ID, ZIP code, city, and state.

2. Orders :-  Stores order information such as order ID, customer ID, order status, purchase date, approval date, delivery dates, and estimated delivery date.

3. Order Items:- Stores the products included in each order, along with seller ID, product ID, price, freight value, and shipping date.

4. Products:- Stores product information such as product ID, product category, product weight, length, height, and width.

5. Sellers:- Stores seller information such as seller ID, ZIP code, city, and state.

6. Payments:- Stores payment information for each order, such as payment type, payment installments, and payment value.

7. Reviews:- Stores customer review information such as review score, review title, review message, and review dates.

8. Geolocation:- Stores location information such as ZIP code, latitude, longitude, city, and state.

9. Product Category Translation:- Converts Portuguese product category names into English category names.

## Step 4: Identify Primary Keys and Foreign Keys.

## Primary Keys and Foreign Keys

| Table | Primary Key | Foreign Key |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | customer_id | — |
| Orders | order_id | customer_id |
| Order Items | order_id + order_item_id | order_id, product_id, seller_id |
| Products | product_id | — |
| Sellers | seller_id | — |
| Payments | order_id + payment_sequential | order_id |
| Reviews | review_id + order_id | order_id |
| Geolocation | No unique primary key | — |
| Category Translation | product_category_name | — |

## Relationships Between Tables

The 9 datasets are connected using common keys.

- Customers and Orders are connected using  = "customer_id"
- Orders and Order Items are connected using = "order_id"
- Order Items and Products are connected using = "product_id"
- Order Items and Sellers are connected using = "seller_id"
- Orders and Payments are connected using = "order_id"
- Orders and Reviews are connected using = "order_id"
- Products and Product Category Translation are connected using = "product_category_name"
- Customers and Sellers can be connected with Geolocation using ZIP code prefix.

## Step 11: Business Insights & Recommendations

### 1. Sales Performance

**Observation:** Revenue and sales are concentrated in a few high-performing product categories and locations.  
**Interpretation:** Customer demand is stronger in specific segments.  
**Business Impact:** Focus inventory and marketing on high-performing categories and regions.

### 2. Customer Behavior

**Observation:** Repeat customers are fewer than new customers.  
**Interpretation:** Customer retention can be improved.  
**Business Impact:** Use loyalty programs and personalized offers to encourage repeat purchases.

### 3. Seller Performance

**Observation:** Some sellers generate much higher orders and revenue than others.  
**Interpretation:** Seller performance is uneven across the platform.  
**Business Impact:** Support top sellers and help lower-performing sellers improve.

### 4. Delivery Performance

**Observation:** Most orders are delivered on time, but some orders are delayed.  
**Interpretation:** Delivery delays can negatively affect customer experience.  
**Business Impact:** Improve logistics and monitor locations with frequent delays.

### 5. Customer Satisfaction

**Observation:** Delayed orders tend to receive lower review scores.  
**Interpretation:** Delivery experience influences customer satisfaction.  
**Business Impact:** Reducing delivery delays can help improve customer ratings and retention.
